Bio & Tech University Guide

A roadmap for an IB student aiming for 38–42 points in DP2: English-taught bachelors across Europe in biomedicine/genetics (Group 1) and tech/AI/CS (Group 2), Swedish-taught engineering (Group 3), and the merit-conversion rules behind all Swedish admissions (Group 4). Assumed start: autumn 2029.

Group 1 · Bio & Biomedicine, Taught in English

Profile & Timeline

WhenWhat to do
M10 (now – Jun 2027)Choose Biology HL, Chemistry HL, Mathematics AA, plus English and a language/humanities subject.
DP1 (2027/28)Biology/chemistry Extended Essay; open days; shortlist 6–8 programmes; sit the SweSAT once as a trial.
DP2 autumn (2028)UK: UCAS (Oxbridge mid-Oct, others late Jan). Netherlands: 15 Jan (capped) / 1 May. Ireland: CAO, 1 Feb. Sweden: 15 April round (see Group 4).
May–Jul 2029IB exams and results, offers confirmed, housing and CSN.

Corrected: Sweden’s two rounds

Karolinska’s own page says the first round (to 15 Jan) needs completed upper-secondary studies, so a student still in DP2 applies in the second round (15 March–15 April), open to Swedish and EU/EEA citizens. SweSAT only counts in the second round.

Where 38–42 lands: 38–39 → Karolinska, Maastricht, UCD, Edinburgh, UCL/Imperial borderline. 40–42 → Oxford, Imperial, UCL, Cambridge (typically 7,7,7 HL), Trinity Genetics.

Tips

  • Chemistry HL is non-negotiable; Biology HL is required by Maastricht. Maths AA beats AI.
  • UK offers read specific HL grades: a 7,6,6 at three HLs beats a 6,5,5 at four.
  • Build the shortlist backwards from deadlines (Oxbridge mid-Oct, Dutch capped programmes 15 Jan).
  • Take the SweSAT once in DP1: low cost, valid for years, and it counts in the April round. It is given in Swedish.
  • Biomedicine is not medicine: none of these degrees give a medical licence.

Group 2 · Tech, AI & Computer Science

Profile & Timeline

Subject choices

Mathematics AA HL is effectively mandatory for top programmes; Computer Science HL or Physics HL is a strong plus. UK Oxbridge/Imperial need admissions tests (MAT, TMUA) in DP2 autumn. At 38–42 points, a 7 in Maths HL matters more than the total.

WhenWhat to do
M10Pick Maths AA HL, Computer Science or Physics HL; start coding projects.
DP1Hackathons, a public GitHub portfolio, SweSAT trial.
DP2MAT/TMUA preparation; UCAS; Dutch capped programmes 15 Jan; Sweden’s April round.

Tips

  • Do not take Maths AI HL if aiming for Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, Delft or ETH.
  • MAT and TMUA are much harder than the IB syllabus: start past papers the summer before DP2.
  • Show, don’t tell: link a live project or repo in applications and your CV.

Group 3 · Taught in Swedish Only

Swedish Language Rules

Swedish-taught programmes require Swedish 3. For IB candidates your draft gives two routes: IB Language A in Swedish (grade 4 or higher) or the Tisus test. English 6 is normally met through the IB. Confirm the grade threshold on universityadmissions.se’s IB page. Fluent Swedish makes this group a genuine option, with the same Swedish quotas (BI) as everyone else.

Group 4 · Merits & Conversions

IB Points → Swedish Merit

IB totals convert to a preliminary merit value on a 10.00–20.00 scale. Foreign-diploma students then receive merit-point compensation of up to 2.5 (the full 2.50 applies at preliminary 19.00–20.00), for a maximum of 22.50.

Your draft’s table (e.g. 38 = 19.38, 40 = 19.70) did not match the antagning.se table above, and 43 points have been needed for 20.00 for years, not only since June 2025. The compensation column assumes the full 2.5, which I derived from the published rule; confirm on UHR’s current table.

BI, BII, HP & Rounds

ItemWhat it means
BIFinal upper-secondary grades (your IB). Main quota.
BIIGrades improved after graduation. Not needed unless retaking.
HPSweSAT, scored 0.00–2.00, ranked separately; written in Swedish; counts in the second round.
First round16 Oct–15 Jan: completed studies only; selection by grades.
Second round15 Mar–15 Apr: Swedish and EU/EEA citizens, including final-year students; grades plus SweSAT; documentation deadline 21 June (check how IB results are submitted).
